Project Overview

This dashboard presents a comprehensive operational intelligence system for analyzing quick-commerce delivery ecosystems across multiple companies and urban regions. The platform combines business analytics, infrastructure analysis, customer insights, and predictive modeling to evaluate delivery efficiency, dark-store utilization, operational load, and customer satisfaction.

The system identifies overloaded operational regions, infrastructure gaps, and delivery bottlenecks while providing actionable business recommendations for improving quick-commerce performance and scalability.

  • Dataset Size: 45,301 real-world quick-commerce orders across multiple companies and urban regions.
  • Core Analytics: Operational load analysis, dark-store utilization, delivery efficiency, customer satisfaction, and demand intelligence.
  • Machine Learning Models: Linear Regression for delivery-time forecasting and Logistic Regression for discount prediction.
  • Business Objective: Identify infrastructure gaps, overloaded regions, and optimization opportunities for scalable quick-commerce operations.

Why This Matters

Quick-commerce platforms operate in highly time-sensitive environments where delivery efficiency, infrastructure allocation, and operational scalability directly impact customer satisfaction and revenue generation.

This dashboard enables businesses to identify overloaded delivery regions, optimize dark-store allocation, improve operational efficiency, and understand customer behavior patterns through data-driven decision-making.

By integrating operational analytics with predictive modeling, the system supports scalable and efficient quick-commerce ecosystem management.

📌 Business Recommendations

🟦 Blinkit

  • Expand dark-store infrastructure in high-demand areas such as Whitefield and Banashankari.
  • Optimize delivery routing during peak operational load to maintain customer satisfaction.
  • Maintain current customer-rating performance through faster fulfillment operations.

🟧 Dunzo

  • Improve operational efficiency in overloaded regions including Rajajinagar and Whitefield.
  • Focus on reducing average delivery time to improve customer ratings.
  • Introduce additional dark stores in high-demand zones to balance order distribution.

🟩 Swiggy Instamart

  • Delivery times remain comparatively higher despite moderate operational load.
  • Improve traffic-aware routing and inventory allocation strategies.
  • Increase dark-store capacity in Marathahalli and Rajajinagar to improve delivery efficiency.

🟪 Zepto

  • Zepto demonstrates strong delivery efficiency even under higher operational demand.
  • Current dark-store utilization strategy appears effective and scalable.
  • Continue expanding in high-demand urban areas while maintaining delivery speed standards.

🔥 Company Insights

📊 Dashboard Insights

  • Customer Rating Analysis
  • Blinkit shows the highest average customer satisfaction among all companies.
  • Dunzo records comparatively lower ratings, indicating potential delivery or service inefficiencies.
  • Customer ratings generally improve with faster delivery performance.

🏬 Dark Store Infrastructure Analysis

  • Several regions show significantly higher demand per dark store, indicating infrastructure strain.
  • Rajajinagar, Whitefield, and Banashankari emerge as major high-demand operational zones.
  • Companies with limited dark-store presence experience higher delivery pressure.

🚚 Operational Efficiency Analysis

  • Higher demand-per-store ratios generally correspond to increased delivery times.
  • Zepto maintains relatively lower delivery times despite higher operational load, suggesting efficient infrastructure utilization.
  • Swiggy Instamart shows comparatively higher delivery times under moderate demand conditions.

⏰ Peak Hour Analysis

  • Delivery times slightly increase during peak operational hours due to higher order density and traffic conditions.
  • Non-peak hours contribute a larger portion of overall orders, reflecting the anytime-demand nature of quick commerce platforms.

📈 Revenue & Demand Trends

  • High-demand regions generate significantly greater operational pressure on existing infrastructure.
  • Companies with better dark-store distribution achieve improved delivery efficiency and customer satisfaction.
  • Operational optimization directly impacts revenue potential and service quality.

📌 Project Conclusion

This project successfully analyzes quick-commerce operational performance using real-world business metrics such as delivery efficiency, customer ratings, demand distribution, and dark-store infrastructure.

The analysis identifies operational overload regions, infrastructure gaps, and company-wise delivery performance differences across major urban areas. By integrating business analytics with predictive modeling, the system provides actionable recommendations for improving delivery efficiency and customer satisfaction.

The project demonstrates how data science and operational intelligence can support strategic decision-making in modern quick-commerce ecosystems.
